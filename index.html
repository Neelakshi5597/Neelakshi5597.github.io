About

Engineering rehabilitation robots that clinicians want to use

I am a PhD scholar in the joint programme of IIT Madras, Christian Medical College Vellore and SCTIMST, working in the R2D2 Lab at IIT Madras and the BioRehab Lab at CMC Vellore.

My doctoral work is ATOBOT, a portable ankle–toe robot for foot rehabilitation after stroke. I have taken it from a clinical need to a working system: designing the sensing and control electronics, writing the embedded firmware, modelling its behaviour from experimental data, and evaluating it with 40 rehabilitation professionals. The invention is now protected by a granted Indian patent.

I trained as an electrical engineer (B.Tech) and a biomedical engineer (M.E., Jadavpur University). Before my PhD, I spent two years at Cognizant Technology Solutions in client-facing software delivery.

  • Robotic neurorehabilitation
  • Human–robot interaction
